What are animals

Animals are living organisms that live in habitats like freezing polar region, dry deserts, high mountains and charming forests


Types of them

  • Reptiles
  • Mammals
  • Sea animals
  • Amphibians

Reptiles

These animals are cold-blooded (they change body temperature based on the weather)
they have scales as their body covering or shells, and lay eggs.

Mammals

Mammals are warm blooded animals (animals that can't change body temperature), give birth to young ones, have fur, skin or hair.

Sea Animals

Sea animals are animals that live in sea. most of them are cold blooded,have most have scales, most breath through gills and lay eggs.

Amphibians

Amphibians are animals that live in sea and on land. They are cold blooded,have most have moist skin, most breath through gills and lungs, and lay eggs.
